# Architectural Decision Record (ADR) Format

ADRs record key architectural and domain design decisions.

## Template

```markdown
# [Number]. [Title]

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: [proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded]

## Context

What is the context, problem, or decision trigger?

## Decision

What change or constraint are we adopting?

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result?
```
