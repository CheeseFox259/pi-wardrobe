# Canonical Triage Labels

The 5 canonical triage labels:
- `needs-triage`: Newly filed issue awaiting initial classification.
- `needs-info`: Blocked on clarification or repro steps from the reporter.
- `ready-for-agent`: Fully specified and scoped for autonomous agent execution.
- `ready-for-human`: Requires human judgment, credentials, or review.
- `wontfix`: Out of scope or intentionally rejected.
